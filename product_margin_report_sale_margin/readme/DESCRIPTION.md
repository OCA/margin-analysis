This module adds 2 new fields to the Product Margins report: Sales margin
and Sales margin rate.

The report's Total Margin compares every sale with every purchase invoiced
in the selected period, so purchases of units not yet sold also reduce it.
For example, if 100 units were purchased but only 10 have been sold, the
Total Margin can be negative. The Sales margin only charges the cost of the
units invoiced to customers, valued at the average purchase price of the
period.
